# Terms

The factors and terms this plugin reads are Ginnie Mae's own disclosures,
published on its Disclosure Data Download to users who sign in and accept
its terms of use on the download page. Its loan-level terms bar attempts to
re-identify borrowers; this plugin reads no loan-level file. Read Ginnie
Mae's current terms on its site before you use the data; your firm settles
them, and sets the dataset's licence on the Data sources page.

The plugin reads only the files your location serves, and every value it
records or offers stays in your deployment.

diligence; the firm using it does its own. This plugin is not affiliated with
or endorsed by Ginnie Mae.
